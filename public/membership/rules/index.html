AMICA SOHO · MEMBERSHIP
23 Frith Street · Soho, London

Membership Rules & Terms

Key membership conditions from premises licence 19/00938/LIPDPS. The council-approved club rules must also be supplied before activation.

Application and approval

Membership applications are reviewed by AMICA SOHO. Submission is not approval, an entry entitlement, or permission to use the venue. Confirmation of an approved membership will be given separately by management.

Eligibility

Membership applicants must be aged 18 or over and work within the hospitality industry when applying, as required by premises licence condition 37(a). Management must verify eligibility before approval.

Guests and entry

At least 48 hours must elapse between application and approval. After 1am on the days following Monday to Saturday, the licence permits only qualifying club members with up to two bona fide guests per member, and up to five bona fide proprietor guests. There is no new entry or re-entry after 1:30am except persons temporarily leaving to smoke. Maximum licensed customer occupancy is 80, excluding staff.

Conduct

Respectful behaviour is required. Management may refuse entry or suspend privileges for breaches of venue rules or applicable law.

Approval and changes

These are key conditions from premises licence 19/00938/LIPDPS dated 11 September 2026, not a substitute for the club rules lodged with and approved by Westminster City Council. Management must confirm the approved club rules and provide their current version before membership is activated. Substantial changes to the approved rules require licensing authority approval.

Questions

For a copy of the current approved rules or questions about your application, email info@amicasoho.com.
