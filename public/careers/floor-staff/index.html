About the role

As part of the floor team, you'll help guests settle in, look after tables and work closely with colleagues to make evenings run smoothly.

What you'll do

  • Welcome guests and provide clear, friendly service throughout their visit.
  • Look after tables, take or assist with orders and respond to guest requests.
  • Coordinate with bar and other floor colleagues during service.
  • Keep guest areas clean, tidy and ready for the next arrival.
  • Support the team in delivering attentive, calm hospitality during busy periods.

What we're looking for

  • A friendly, reliable and professional manner.
  • Confident communication with guests and team members.
  • Attention to detail and a willingness to help during busy service.
  • Tell us about any hospitality or customer-facing experience in your application.

We welcome applications from people who can demonstrate the qualities above. Please describe your relevant hospitality, customer-service or other experience, and your availability.

Location and working hours
