Useful to know

Pre-theatre dining FAQs

Does AMICA have a pre-theatre set menu?

No. Theatre guests can order from our regular à la carte food and drinks menus. Italian small plates start at £4 and desserts are £5.

Where is AMICA Soho?

AMICA is at 23 Frith Street, Soho, London W1D 4RR, near the Prince Edward Theatre, Palace Theatre and other West End venues.

What time should I book before a show?

AMICA opens from 5pm. Allow time to enjoy your food and drinks and walk to the theatre. Tell the team your performance time when making your reservation.

Can I visit for cocktails after the theatre?

Yes, AMICA is a late cocktail lounge and restaurant. Please check availability and closing times for your chosen date.

Are vegetarian and vegan dishes available?

Yes. The menu marks vegetarian, vegan and other dietary options. Please confirm ingredients, allergens and preparation with the team.
